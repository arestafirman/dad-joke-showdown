One dad joke, told at the barbecue:

> My son asked why I bring a pencil to every barbecue. I told him somebody has to draw the line at second helpings.

A son wonders why dad packs a pencil for a barbecue — and the pencil turns out to be there for one job: drawing the line at second helpings.

The joke lands because the pencil is the most useless thing at a grill until the punchline gives it a job — and "draw the line" is both what a pencil does and what a dad does when the potato salad runs low.
